Injection Molding Machine Operating Manual

Problem: Incomplete filling of molded product

Cause:
- Insufficient injection pressure
- Improper barrel or mold temperature
- Poor mold alignment or blockage
- Low-quality or improperly dried raw material

Solution:
1. Increase injection pressure to ensure complete mold filling
2. Adjust barrel and mold temperature to recommended levels
3. Inspect and correct mold alignment and flow paths
4. Verify material quality and ensure proper drying before use
