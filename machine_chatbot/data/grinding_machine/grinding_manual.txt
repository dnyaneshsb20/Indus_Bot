Grinding Machine Operating Manual

Problem: Poor surface finish on the workpiece during grinding operation

Cause:
- Dull or worn-out grinding wheel
- Improper feed rate
- Insufficient or improper coolant flow
- Unbalanced or misaligned grinding wheel

Solution:
1. Dress or replace the grinding wheel to restore sharpness
2. Reduce the feed rate to improve surface quality
3. Apply suitable coolant to reduce heat and friction
4. Check and correct the balance and alignment of the grinding wheel
