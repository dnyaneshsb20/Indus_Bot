Lathe Machine Operating Manual

Problem: Excessive heat generation during lathe operation

Cause:
- High cutting speed or feed rate
- Improper tool geometry or worn cutting tool
- Insufficient lubrication of spindle and moving parts
- Continuous machining without cooling intervals

Solution:
1. Reduce cutting speed and feed rate according to material type
2. Replace or regrind worn cutting tools
3. Ensure proper lubrication of spindle and carriage mechanisms
4. Allow periodic cooling breaks during long machining operations
