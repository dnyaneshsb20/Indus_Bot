Drilling Machine Operating Manual

Problem: Drill bit overheating during operation

Cause:
- Excessive drilling speed
- Dull or worn-out drill bit
- Insufficient coolant or lubrication
- Incorrect feed rate

Solution:
1. Reduce the spindle speed according to material type
2. Replace or sharpen the drill bit
3. Apply proper coolant or cutting fluid
4. Adjust the feed rate to recommended values
5. Ensure proper alignment of the drill bit
