CNC Machine Operating Manual

Problem: Excessive heat generation during CNC machine operation

Cause:
- High cutting load
- Inadequate lubrication of moving parts
- Blocked cooling fans or ventilation paths
- Continuous operation without cooling intervals

Solution:
1. Inspect and maintain the lubrication system regularly
2. Reduce cutting load and optimize machining parameters
3. Clean cooling fans and ventilation openings
4. Allow periodic cooling breaks during long operations
